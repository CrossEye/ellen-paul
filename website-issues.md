## 8. Wed, Jul 22, 2026, 7:09 AM — Scott Sauyet

This is great stuff!

I'll leave it to Jay to talk about the actual page design, but I want to point out that, in my experience, while there's a very minor difference in initial setup work, there is practically no maintenance difference between a single-page site and a multi-page one. This is to say, don't let this be a deciding factor. Choose single-page or multi-page for reasons of how you want to present the information, not the amount of work. Perhaps Jay's experience is different, though.

I've been focused mostly on anti-Weir messaging that I haven't thought much yet about what your pro-Ellen messaging will look like. This document is very helpful.

But if you want to do compare and contrast, I think you should pick 6 - 8 topics where you can easily distinguish Steve's record from your positions. I attached an image I haven't done anything with yet using scorecard numbers that might be useful. And my oppo stuff is concentrated at https://55th.ahmdems.org/reports/weir-analysis. Trip has the experience to say more definitively, but my thought would be to stay away from things like reproductive rights and gun control. Anyone likely to vote in favor of you on these issues would already choose you as the more progressive candidate, while you don't want a borderline voter to say, "Wait, she wants a three-day waiting period before a five-year old can buy an Uzi? Hard no!" 😄 (Quiz: what am I quoting?)
